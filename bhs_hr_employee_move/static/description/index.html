Key Value

Employee Transfer Management là tiện ích mở rộng giúp quản lý và theo dõi việc điều chuyển nhân sự nội bộ, bao gồm luân chuyển phòng ban và chuyển đổi thực tập sinh thành nhân viên chính thức. Perfect for: HR Teams, Managers & Team Leaders

Key Features

Transfer Record Menu

Features a new “Transfer Record” menu in Odoo to manage employee movement history, including Department Transfer and Intern-to-Employee conversion.

Employees Transfer Department

When an employee is transferred to another department, users execute the department transfer action on the employee form. The system automatically creates a record with: employees, old department, new department, date transferred, and saves it as transfer history.

Intern Upgrade To Employee

When an intern is upgraded to a full-time employee, users execute the convert to employee action on the employee record. The system automatically creates a conversion history with: intern, department, date upgraded, and stores it in the upgrade history.
